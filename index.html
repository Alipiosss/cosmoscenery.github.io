Cosmoscenery Analytics exists to close that gap — combining deep operational expertise with modern data tools to build decision systems that actually change how companies are managed.

Why Your BI Dashboard Isn't Being Used — And What To Do About It

Most Power BI implementations fail quietly. The dashboards get built, the data gets connected, and then six months later nobody's looking at them. The problem is almost never technical. It's operational. It's the gap between what a tool can show and what a management team actually needs to decide. Here's how to close it.

The root cause, in most cases, is that KPIs were defined by whoever was closest to the data — not by whoever was accountable for the decisions. A financial controller builds metrics that make sense to a financial controller. A CEO needs to know whether to hire, whether to expand, whether to slow down. Those are different questions.
