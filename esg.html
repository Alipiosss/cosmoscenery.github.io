// The Three Pillars

Environmental. Social. Governance.
All three. Done right.

We don't cherry-pick the easy metrics. We build a complete ESG framework that covers all three dimensions — with the depth that investors and regulators actually require.

E

Environmental

  • Carbon footprint measurement (Scope 1, 2 & 3)
  • Energy consumption and renewable sourcing
  • Water usage and efficiency tracking
  • Waste generation and circular economy metrics
  • Biodiversity and land use impact
  • Climate risk assessment and scenario analysis
S

Social

  • Employee health, safety, and wellbeing
  • Gender pay gap and diversity metrics
  • Training, development, and retention rates
  • Supply chain labour standards
  • Community impact and local employment
  • Human rights due diligence
G

Governance

  • Board composition and independence
  • Executive compensation transparency
  • Anti-corruption and ethics policies
  • Data privacy and cybersecurity governance
  • Stakeholder engagement processes
  • Sustainability strategy and accountability
