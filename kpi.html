// KPI Library

Examples by business area.

These are illustrative examples of the KPIs we design and implement. Your framework will be tailored to your specific strategy, sector, and management needs.

On-Time Delivery Rate
Orders delivered on time / Total orders × 100
Measures operational reliability. A leading indicator of client satisfaction and repeat business risk.
Process Cycle Time
End date − Start date (average across processes)
Tracks how long key operational processes take. Reduction signals efficiency gains and capacity improvements.
Defect / Error Rate
Defective outputs / Total outputs × 100
Quality control indicator. High rates signal process breakdowns before they reach clients.
Capacity Utilisation
Actual output / Maximum possible output × 100
Identifies underutilised or overloaded resources — essential for hiring and investment decisions.
Gross Margin
(Revenue − COGS) / Revenue × 100
Core profitability indicator. Tracked by product line or service to identify high and low performers.
Cash Conversion Cycle
DIO + DSO − DPO
Measures how efficiently working capital is managed. Critical for growth-stage SMEs managing cash flow.
Revenue per Employee
Total Revenue / Number of Employees
Productivity benchmark. Tracked over time to assess whether headcount growth is generating proportional output.
Budget Variance
(Actual − Budget) / Budget × 100
Early warning for cost overruns. Tracked monthly by department to maintain financial discipline.
Customer Acquisition Cost
Total Sales & Marketing spend / New customers acquired
Measures efficiency of commercial investment. Compared against LTV to assess growth sustainability.
Client Retention Rate
(Clients at end − New clients) / Clients at start × 100
Leading indicator of service quality and relationship health. More valuable than acquisition in B2B.
Pipeline Conversion Rate
Deals closed / Total pipeline opportunities × 100
Sales effectiveness indicator. Tracked by stage to identify where deals are being lost.
Net Revenue Retention
(Starting MRR + Expansion − Churn) / Starting MRR × 100
Growth quality metric. Above 100% means existing clients drive revenue growth without new acquisition.
Carbon Intensity
Total CO₂e emissions / Revenue (€M)
Normalised environmental performance. Required for CSRD reporting and investor ESG assessments.
Energy Consumption per Unit
Total kWh / Units produced or services delivered
Operational efficiency and sustainability indicator. Tracked to identify reduction opportunities.
Gender Pay Gap
Avg male salary − Avg female salary / Avg male salary × 100
Social equity metric. Increasingly required by investors, regulators, and public funding bodies.
Supplier Sustainability Score
Weighted average of supplier ESG assessments
Supply chain risk metric. Essential for companies with Scope 3 reporting obligations.
Employee Net Promoter Score
% Promoters − % Detractors
Culture and engagement indicator. Strong predictor of retention, productivity, and client satisfaction.
Voluntary Turnover Rate
Voluntary exits / Avg headcount × 100 (annualised)
Talent retention indicator. High rates signal management, compensation, or culture issues before they escalate.
Training Hours per Employee
Total training hours / Total employees
Capability development metric. Linked to innovation capacity and long-term competitiveness.
Absenteeism Rate
Days absent / Total working days × 100
Wellbeing and engagement proxy. Early signal of burnout or management issues before they affect output.
